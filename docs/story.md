# Surface Tension — the story

One page, one story about Shifat Masud, told in water.
**Who he is → how he thinks → what he has built → how it scales → where he goes deep → where he is going → say hi.**

The thread that ties it together: *hard precision resting on something soft.* Shifat is a design engineer: the facets are code, systems and maths; the water is feel, motion and physics. The page never says this. It shows it, once per section, with one star.

Motion grammar (processing fluency): every reveal uses the same mask slide-up (expo.out, 1.2–1.8 s, 0.12 s line stagger); every camera move is spring-damped (f 0.9, ζ 1); the scroll is one predictable column, pinned only where a scene needs time.

---

## 0 · Intro — the first drop
- **Beat:** a counter runs 000→100 on real loading progress; at 100 one drop falls.
- **Idea:** something is about to be disturbed.
- **Star:** the drop.
- **Copy:** none except the counter (display size). The number is honest, so it is calm.
- **Hook:** the drop hits, the first ring opens the hero.
- **Principles:** curiosity gap (what is the drop falling into?), goal-gradient (the counter).

## 1 · Surface (hero) — who he is
- **Beat:** a calm golden-hour lake. A blue brilliant-cut glass diamond hovers on it; every few seconds its tip kisses the water and a soft ring travels out. Your cursor is a fingertip on the water.
- **Idea:** precision floating on something fluid. That is the job.
- **Star:** the diamond (the only saturated blue object on screen). It sits on the right-third line with the horizon on the upper third; the name owns the left column.
- **Copy (adds what the visual can't):** "Shifat Masud" (body), "Design engineer & creative technologist — Dhaka" (small). No adjective about water: the water is already there.
- **Hook:** scroll and the camera cranes up slowly until the diamond is a single point of blue on a huge surface and its last ring is still travelling outward. The ring is unresolved; it carries into the statement.
- **Principles:** Von Restorff (one distinctive object), figure-ground (gem vs. water), Fitts (the whole surface is the interaction target), peak-end is *not* spent here: calm first.

## 2 · Statement — how he thinks
- **Beat:** top-down over the water, the ring still expanding. Three display-size lines surface one at a time and sink away.
- **Idea:** he treats the interface as a physical material.
- **Star:** one display phrase at a time (giant type vs. tiny label).
- **Copy:** "Pixels with mass." / "Motion with memory." / "Systems that ship." Each with one small specific line: hand-written springs, buoyancy and damping; second-order motion that remembers its velocity; a design system (Shade DS) and the tooling that ships it.
- **Hook:** as the last line sinks, the ring reaches the frame edge and a card breaks the surface.
- **Principles:** chunking (one idea per viewport, ≤ 3 sizes), Zeigarnik (the ring is finished only when the lab begins), progressive disclosure.

## 3 · Lab — what he has built
- **Beat:** a horizontal shelf of experiments floating in the water. Vertical scroll moves them; they bend with scroll speed; each reel is grey at rest and blooms into colour as it reaches the centre (or on hover/focus).
- **Idea:** these are real, running things, not mockups.
- **Star:** the centred card (one at a time is in colour).
- **Copy:** title, year, three tags, one line on what it does that the reel can't show (e.g. "your webcam is the controller").
- **Order (serial position):** Water Sim first (strongest, and on theme), Jelly Drag last; the quieter pieces in the middle.
- **Hook:** the last card is the jelly; when you leave it, its wobble keeps going and becomes the pucks in the next section.
- **Principles:** serial position, variable reward (colour bloom on arrival), common fate (cards move together, no borders), Hick (one card in focus).

## 4 · Systems — how it scales
- **Beat:** a tray of real UI parts (button, toggle, slider, chip) as soft pucks you can press; they squash and spring back; the toggle really toggles.
- **Idea:** the craft becomes a system other people can use (Shade DS, his MCP servers and the Upsy OAuth bridge).
- **Star:** the toggle.
- **Copy:** what the system is and who it is for, in two short chunks. The pucks prove the "feel"; the words only name the system.
- **Hook:** flipping the toggle (or scrolling on) dims the light; the surface tilts up and we go under.
- **Principles:** aesthetic-usability (it feels good, so it reads as well made), Fitts (big targets), Gestalt proximity (the tray groups the parts, no frame).

## 5 · Dive — where he goes deep
- **Beat:** the camera breaks through the surface: Snell's window overhead, god rays, a slow drift of silt, caustics on the sand. His tools drift by as crisp labels at different depths.
- **Idea:** the depth under the polished surface: shaders, physics, GPU.
- **Star:** the light coming through the surface (Snell's window).
- **Copy:** one display line, "Under the surface", then only the tool names (small). The light says the rest.
- **Hook:** we keep sinking until the water is black and the silt turns into stars: the deep becomes space. A small red figure drifts into frame.
- **Principles:** continuity (water → space), figure-ground (labels on the dark), curiosity gap (who is that?).

## 6 · Journey — where he is going (the peak)
- **Beat:** a red crewmate drifts through deep space, descends, lands in a green forest. The camera pulls back: it was all playing on a tablet. Then the crewmate breaks out through the screen as a water-like rupture, into the page.
- **Idea:** his work is meant to be played with, and it does not stay inside the screen.
- **Star:** the crewmate, every beat.
- **Copy:** two small lines at most, timed to beats: one in space, one at the reveal.
- **Hook:** the break-out throws droplets forward that land on the contact section's water.
- **Principles:** peak-end rule (this is the one emotional peak), surprise / variable reward (the tablet reveal), Zeigarnik (the break resolves in the CTA).

## 7 · Contact — say hi (the end)
- **Beat:** back on bright water; the droplets from the break-out land and ring out. One big button.
- **Idea:** start something together.
- **Star:** the button (largest target on the page).
- **Copy:** a display line, his live Dhaka time (small), links. A form for those who prefer it.
- **Hook / end:** overscroll fills a bar and floats you back to the surface, where the diamond is still breathing.
- **Principles:** peak-end (a memorable, calm end), Hick (one action), Fitts (huge target), goal-gradient (the right-edge water level fills as you approach the end).
